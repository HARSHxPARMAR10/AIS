# AIDEX Case Lifecycle

## Introduction

The Case Lifecycle describes every stage of a cyber crime investigation from case creation to archival.

It ensures that all investigation activities are properly documented and traceable.

---

## Case Lifecycle Diagram

```text
Create
      ↓
Assign
      ↓
Investigate
      ↓
Upload Evidence
      ↓
Evidence Verification
      ↓
AI Analysis
      ↓
Generate Report
      ↓
Close Case
      ↓
Archive
```

---

## Stage 1 – Create

A new cyber crime case is created by the Administrator or Investigator.

---

## Stage 2 – Assign

The case is assigned to an authorized investigator.

---

## Stage 3 – Investigate

The investigator collects information and begins the investigation.

---

## Stage 4 – Upload Evidence

Digital evidence related to the investigation is uploaded into the system.

---

## Stage 5 – Evidence Verification

The uploaded evidence is verified using metadata validation and SHA-256 hash verification.

---

## Stage 6 – AI Analysis

The AI engine analyzes the uploaded evidence and generates intelligent findings.

---

## Stage 7 – Generate Report

The investigator prepares the final cyber crime investigation report.

---

## Stage 8 – Close Case

After successful investigation, the case status is changed to Closed.

---

## Stage 9 – Archive

Closed cases are archived for future reference and legal purposes.

---

## Lifecycle Benefits

- Organized Investigation
- Better Case Tracking
- Secure Documentation
- AI Integration
- Complete Audit Trail
- Easy Report Generation

---

## Expected Result

Every cyber crime case should follow a standardized lifecycle from creation to archival while maintaining security, integrity and complete investigation history.
