# AIDEX Evidence Workflow

## Introduction

The Evidence Workflow defines the complete lifecycle of digital evidence inside the AIDEX system.

---

## Evidence Workflow Diagram

```text
Evidence Collection
        ↓
Evidence Upload
        ↓
Evidence Verification
        ↓
AI Analysis
        ↓
Evidence Approval
        ↓
Evidence Storage
        ↓
Case Report
```

---

## Step 1 – Evidence Collection

Digital evidence is collected from the crime scene or digital devices.

---

## Step 2 – Evidence Upload

The investigator uploads evidence into the AIDEX system.

---

## Step 3 – Evidence Verification

The uploaded evidence is verified using:

- SHA-256 Hash
- File Metadata
- Duplicate Detection

---

## Step 4 – AI Analysis

AI analyses the uploaded evidence and generates useful findings.

---

## Step 5 – Evidence Approval

The Cyber Officer verifies and approves the evidence.

---

## Step 6 – Evidence Storage

Approved evidence is securely stored in the database and media storage.

---

## Step 7 – Case Report

Evidence is used for generating investigation reports.

---

## Expected Result

Every piece of digital evidence should follow a secure and traceable workflow.
