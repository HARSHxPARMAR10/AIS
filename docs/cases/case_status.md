# AIDEX Case Status Management

## Introduction

Case Status represents the current progress of a cyber crime investigation.

Each case moves through predefined stages until the investigation is completed.

---

## Available Status

The system supports:

- Open
- Assigned
- Under Investigation
- Evidence Uploaded
- AI Analysis Completed
- Report Generated
- Closed
- Archived

---

## Status Flow

```text
Open
    ↓
Assigned
    ↓
Under Investigation
    ↓
Evidence Uploaded
    ↓
AI Analysis Completed
    ↓
Report Generated
    ↓
Closed
    ↓
Archived
```

---

## Status Description

### Open

Case has been created.

---

### Assigned

Case has been assigned to an investigator.

---

### Under Investigation

Investigation is currently in progress.

---

### Evidence Uploaded

Required evidence has been uploaded.

---

### AI Analysis Completed

AI has completed evidence analysis.

---

### Report Generated

Investigation report has been prepared.

---

### Closed

Investigation has been successfully completed.

---

### Archived

Closed case has been moved to archive storage.

---

## Expected Result

Every cyber crime case should move through predefined investigation stages in a secure and organized manner.
