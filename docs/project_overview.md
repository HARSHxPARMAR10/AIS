# AIDEX Project Overview

## Introduction

AIDEX (AI Digital Evidence Intelligence & Investigation System) is an AI-powered cyber crime evidence management platform developed as a GTU Diploma Minor Project.

The system helps investigators securely collect, manage, verify, and analyze digital evidence.

---

## Objectives

- Digital Evidence Management

- Secure Storage

- AI Based Analysis

- OCR Integration

- Hash Verification

- Investigation Management

---

## Main Modules

- Authentication

- Dashboard

- Case Management

- Evidence Management

- AI Engine

- Reports
